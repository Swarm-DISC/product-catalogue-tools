Batch-wide issues (all registered records)
  • §1 Every non-ESA creator is registered with nameType="Personal"; it should be "Organizational".
  • §2 No creator carries its ROR nameIdentifier (only the publisher has ESA's ROR).
  • The form has no nameType or ROR input, so issues §1 and §2 cannot be fixed through this form — and per schema_org_handoff.md §6, landing-page harvesting cannot carry creator ROR either. The routes that work are the "Creator ROR" spreadsheet column proposed in datacite_metadata_issues.md §2, or a DataCite API push of the DataCite JSON below.
